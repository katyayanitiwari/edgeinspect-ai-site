Severity engine

Each detected defect is graded by its size compared with defects of the same class in the training data. These thresholds are a data-based starting point, not an industrial standard; a real product would use its own QC specification.

ClassTrain boxesMajor ≥ area %Critical ≥ area %
crazing48321.138.0
inclusion6914.118.0
patches6139.428.2
pitted_surface30055.697.0
rolled-in_scale43312.428.1
scratches3827.516.1
  1. Detections below 0.25 confidence are ignored.
  2. Box area above the class median is major, above the class 90th percentile is critical, otherwise minor.
  3. The part takes the worst severity. 3 or more defects raise it one level.
  4. A confident critical defect means REJECT. Any defect below 0.5 confidence sends the part to MANUAL REVIEW. Otherwise major means REWORK and minor means PASS WITH NOTE.

Dataset

NEU-DET hot-rolled steel strip surface defects: 1,800 images found, 1 exact duplicate removed, 0 corrupted files, 0 invalid boxes. Stratified split with seed 42. Boxes per class: crazing 689, patches 881, inclusion 1,011, pitted_surface 432, rolled-in_scale 628, scratches 548.
